KeymaHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the KeymaHub Android app (“the app”) handles information. The app is developed by an individual developer, pqzmggg (“we”).

In short: KeymaHub does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The app does not even have permission to access the internet. Keyboard and mouse input goes only to the Bluetooth device you choose.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or have access to any information about you or your devices. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else over the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Accessibility service

KeymaHub uses the Android accessibility service API, and you turn it on yourself after reading an explanation in the app. It is used only to:

  • Receive input from the keyboard and mouse connected to this device.
  • Send that input over Bluetooth to the device (PC, tablet and so on) you select with a hotkey. While this device is selected, input is left as it is.
  • While another device is being controlled: read the text of a text field you touch on this device, so you can type into it with the on-screen keyboard, and notice when a notification is posted, so the covered screen can be shown again.

Input and screen content are never stored and never sent to the internet or to us. Input only goes to the device you select. Apart from the cases above, the app does not read screen content.

Other permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect and advertise): to let your PC or tablet find this device as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse, pair with it and connect to it.
  • Notifications: to show the ongoing notification while hosting is on, and connection status.
  • Ignore battery optimization (optional): to keep connections alive while the screen is off.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device: names and Bluetooth addresses of paired devices, the nicknames you give them, profiles, hotkeys and settings. App backup is turned off, so this information is not copied to cloud backups. A short diagnostic log is kept in memory only and is cleared when the app stops. You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any information to third parties. The only place your input goes is the Bluetooth device you paired and selected.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
