KeymaHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the KeymaHub application for Android (the "Application"), developed by pqzmggg (the "Developer"), handles information in connection with its use.

1. Information Collected

The Developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any personal information of users. The Application operates no servers, requires no account, and does not request the Android permission to access the internet. Accordingly, the Application is unable to transmit any information to the Developer or to any other party over the internet. The Application contains no advertising, analytics, or crash reporting components.

2. Use of the Accessibility Service

The Application uses the Android Accessibility Service API. The service is enabled by the user after the purpose of its use has been presented within the Application. The service is used solely for the following purposes:

  1. To receive input from a keyboard and mouse connected to the device on which the Application is installed.
  2. To transmit such input over Bluetooth to the device selected by the user by means of a hotkey. While the user's own device is selected, input is not altered.
  3. While another device is being controlled, to read the text of an input field that the user touches on the device, so that the user may enter text into that field with the on-screen keyboard, and to detect that a notification has been posted, so that the covered screen may be displayed again.

Input and screen content obtained through the service are neither stored nor transmitted to the internet or to the Developer. Input is delivered only to the device selected by the user. Except as described above, the Application does not read screen content.

3. Permissions and Their Purposes

  1. Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect and advertise): to allow a computer or tablet to discover the device as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse, and to pair and connect with it.
  2. Notifications: to display an ongoing notification while hosting is active, together with the connection status.
  3. Exemption from battery optimization (optional): to maintain connections while the screen is turned off.

4. Information Stored on the Device and Its Deletion

For its operation, the Application retains the names and Bluetooth addresses of paired devices, the names assigned to them by the user, profiles, hotkeys, and settings, solely in the Application's private storage on the user's device. Application backup is disabled, and this information is therefore not copied to any cloud backup. Diagnostic records are kept in memory only and are erased when the Application stops. The user may delete all such information at any time by clearing the Application's storage or by uninstalling the Application.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Developer does not provide, sell, or transfer any information to third parties. Input is delivered only to the Bluetooth device that the user has paired and selected.

6. Children

The Application is not directed at children and does not collect information from any person, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any amendment to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page together with its effective date.

8. Contact

Inquiries concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to pqzmggg@gmail.com.
